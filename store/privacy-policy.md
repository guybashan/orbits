# Privacy Policy — Orbits

**Last updated: 11 August 2026**

## The short version

Orbits does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There is
no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection.

## What the app stores

Orbits saves your game progress — which levels you have finished, the stars and
best scores you earned, and whether you have sound, music and vibration turned
on. This is written to the app's private storage on your own device using
Godot's `user://` save file.

It never leaves your device. It is not backed up to us, because there is no
"us" to back it up to — the app has no server.

Uninstalling Orbits deletes it.

## What the app does not do

- No personal information is requested or collected. No name, no email, no
  phone number, no location.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers are read.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No network requests. The game is fully playable offline because it never
  needs to be online.

## Permissions

Orbits requests one Android permission:

- **VIBRATE** — used only for haptic feedback when a ball settles into place.
  You can turn it off in Settings. It grants no access to any information.

## Children

Orbits is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it
collects no data from children either.

## Changes

If a future version of Orbits ever collects anything, this policy will be
updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here
rather than quietly amended.
